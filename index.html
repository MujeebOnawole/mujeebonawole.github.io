Abdulmujeeb T. Onawole, PhD

Hello, I am a computational chemist and explainable AI specialist based in Brisbane, Australia. My research applies quantum chemistry and machine learning across life science and material science, with work spanning antimicrobial drug discovery and carbon capture. A central priority is explainability, building AI models whose predictions chemists can trust and act on. I completed my PhD in Computational Chemistry at The University of Queensland.
